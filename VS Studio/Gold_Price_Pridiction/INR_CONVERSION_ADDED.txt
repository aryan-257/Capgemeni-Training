💱 CONVERSION DETAILS:
================================================================================

Exchange Rate: 1 USD = ₹83.12 (configurable)
Weight Conversion: 1 Troy Ounce = 31.1035 grams

Formula:
- Price per gram (INR) = (USD per ounce × Exchange Rate) ÷ 31.1035
- Price per 10 grams = Price per gram × 10
- Price per kg = Price per gram × 1000
